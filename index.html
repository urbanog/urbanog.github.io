About me

I am a young researcher at the Institute of Mathematics, Physics and Mechanics in Ljubljana and a doctoral student at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, University of Ljubljana, where I am a member of the Topology and its Applications group.

I work in geometric and algebraic topology. My main interest is topological complexity and its relatives: invariants that measure how many continuous rules are needed to plan motion in a space, and how these numbers are controlled by the fundamental group, its actions and its cohomology. I am also interested in configuration spaces and graph braid groups.
