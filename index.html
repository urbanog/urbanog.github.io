Research

I work in algebraic topology. Most of my work concerns topological complexity and its relatives. The topological complexity of a space measures how many continuous rules a robot needs to move between any two positions in that space. The Lusternik–Schnirelmann category asks a related question: how many contractible open sets are needed to cover the space. Both are special cases of the sectional category of a map.

I study parametrized versions of these invariants, where the space varies over a base and the motion rules must vary with it. For aspherical spaces they become questions about groups, their actions and their cohomology. I am also interested in configuration spaces and graph braid groups.
